🤖 AI chaos squad: 3 agents · 3 models · 1 error each

Each agent runs on a different local model and chooses one error of its type on a different service. They take turns choosing (one model fits the GPU at a time), then all three errors are injected together and held until Rollback. After 30 s of live telemetry the analyst model ranks them by severity and recommends fixes.
